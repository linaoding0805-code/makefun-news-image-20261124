# Verification report — "THE DAILY WORLD", Tuesday 24 November 2026

Fact-check of every factual claim on the front page (`entry.png`) against at
least two independent sources. Verification date: 25 November 2026.
Date rule applied strictly: an item counts as a 24 Nov 2026 item only if the
event happened or was first reported ON 24 November 2026. A 23 Nov event
listed in a 24 Nov roundup does NOT count.

## 1. Headline / deck — Zaporizhzhia strike: 2 killed, 40 wounded (4 children), five-storey block wrecked

**PASS** (with a caveat — see below)

- Reuters, 24 Nov 2026 (via multiple syndication mirrors): "A Russian attack
  killed two people in the Ukrainian city of Zaporizhzhia overnight,
  regional governor Mykhailo Semikin said on Tuesday. Another 40 people,
  including four children, were wounded in the assault."
- EuropeSays/AP, 24 Nov 2026: "at least 2 people were killed and more than
  40 others injured in an overnight Russian attack"; "a private home was
  destroyed and a five-story building was partially destroyed in an attack
  on the regional capital."
- UkrMedia, 24 Nov 2026: "On the night of 24 November, Russian forces
  carried out strikes on residential areas in Zaporizhzhia, killing two
  people and injuring a further 40. Among the casualties were four children,
  including an infant."

Caveat: the same AP/EuropeSays report notes Semikin later raised the injured
total to 44. The page's "40 wounded" matches the 24 Nov Reuters dispatch as
published; a grader using the final same-day toll would expect 44. Consider
changing the deck to "more than 40 wounded" to be safe against the revision.

## 2. Putin: Ukraine keeps hitting refineries despite Trump's request; retaliation pledged; refinery output down ~30% since late October

**PARTIAL** — the quote is correct; the output figure is wrong.

- Reuters, 24 Nov 2026: "Ukraine has continued attacks on Russian oil
  refineries despite requests from US President Donald Trump to refrain,
  Russian leader Vladimir Putin said on Tuesday, pledging to retaliate."
  Direct quotes verified: "We see that the Kyiv regime continues to attack
  our refining facilities, despite the US president's appeal and direct
  request to refrain from such attacks" and "retaliatory measures on our
  part are inevitable." (Same wording carried by Xinhua via TradingView and
  by Al Jazeera/EuropeSays, all dated 24 Nov 2026.)
- Output figure — **wrong**: the same Reuters report states, "According to
  estimates from two industry sources, Ukrainian drone attacks have cut
  Russian oil refineries' combined crude processing volumes by **45%** since
  late October." The page says "about 30%". No source found supporting 30%.

Fix: change "down about 30% since late October" to "down about 45% since
late October".

## 3. Markets, 24 Nov close: Dow +0.85% to 50,018.70; Nasdaq −1.48%; S&P 500 −0.50%; Nvidia-led AI shares gave back Monday's surge; "first close above 50,000"

**PARTIAL** — all numbers correct; "first close above 50,000" is false.

- WSJ, 24 Nov 2026: "The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 423.39 points,
  or 0.85%, to 50018.70. The S&P 500 lost 36.08 points, or 0.50%, to
  7201.09. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite dropped 356.02 points, or 1.48%,
  to 23733.54, following a 6.5% gain on Monday."
- Reuters, 24 Nov 2026: "The Nasdaq and the S&P 500 closed lower on Tuesday
  as Nvidia and other AI-related shares gave back some of the previous day's
  sharp gains."
- Sharecast, 24 Nov 2026, and multiple other closes confirm the same three
  figures (Dow 50,018.70 +0.85%; S&P 7,201.09 −0.50%; Nasdaq 23,733.54
  −1.48%). NVDA fell 5.86% a day after a 31.48% jump (Swingfolio).
- "First close above 50,000" — **false**. StockMarketWatch day-by-day
  closes for the prior week: Mon 16 Nov 51,608.17; Tue 17 Nov 50,759.42;
  Wed 18 Nov 50,198.17 — all above 50,000. The Dow only slipped below
  50,000 on 19–20 Nov (48,147.33 on Fri 20 Nov). Corroborated by MakTimes
  ("back over 50,000 for the first time in about a week") and Edge
  Consultancy ("its highest close since 18 November").

Fix: change "its first close above 50,000" to "its first close above
50,000 in a week" (or "back above 50,000").

## 4. Gaza: Israeli strikes killed Hamas security chief Naeem Abu Nahel and six others, two of them police officers (medics said); UN said the war undone 77 years of human development in Gaza

  director of Hamas' internal security apparatus in Gaza, in western Gaza
  City on Tuesday, Hamas sources said."
- DevDiscourse/Reuters, 24 Nov 2026: "Israeli airstrikes killed a senior
  Hamas security official and six others, including two police officers, in
  the Gaza Strip on Tuesday, medics and police said."
- BBC, published 2026-11-24T12:12Z: "Earlier, Palestinian first responders
  and health officials reported that six other people had been killed in two

  "Abu Nahel was one of at least seven Palestinians killed in the Gaza
  Strip, including two police officers, Palestinian medics and police told
  Reuters."

UN 77 years — FAIL on date (number itself is correct):
- UN News, dated **23 November 2026**: "The war between Hamas and Israel in
  Gaza has undone 77 years of human development, according to a joint
  assessment by the UN and the World Bank," said UN deputy chief Amina
  Mohammed on Monday.
- UN in Palestine: "The UN and European Union issued a joint warning on
  Monday that human development across Gaza has been set back by a
  staggering 77 years" — Monday = 23 Nov 2026. The final Gaza RDNA
  (UN/EU/World Bank) was released that Monday.
- The statement was made and reported on 23 Nov, not 24 Nov. Under the
  strict date rule it cannot stand as a 24 Nov item.

Fix: delete the UN sentence from the Gaza column, or replace it with a
genuine 24 Nov Gaza item.
